# First Communication Compliance: Per-CRM Template Guide (CC 1784.41(a)(3))

Sources: Cal. Civ. Code § 1784.41(a)(3); CNCDA CARS Act Compliance Guide v1.2 (2026-08-07) Part 2 and Appendix D Example 5; CNCDA Webinar FAQ (2026-08-31) Questions 23 to 38. Vault digest: `Resources/automotive-guidelines/cncda-cars-act-guidance.md`, rules `CARS-FWC-01` to `CARS-FWC-15`. Cite CNCDA as "CNCDA FAQ QN".

## The rule in one line

The dealer's first **written** communication with a consumer that references a specific vehicle for sale, or any monetary amount or financing term for any vehicle, must clearly and conspicuously disclose the vehicle's **total price** at least once in the dealer's first response regarding that vehicle, and the dealer must retain that communication for 2 years and produce a copy on the consumer's written request.

## What CNCDA settled (build templates to these)

1. **The trigger is content and context, not channel.** Email, text, chat, digital-retailing message, or a worksheet shown to a walk-in all qualify. A verbal quote does not, and no disclaimer is needed before a phone quote (FAQ Q29).
2. **A reply to a vehicle-specific inquiry is a response regarding that vehicle whether or not it names the vehicle.** A generic acknowledgment, an invitation to apply for credit, or any substantive reply that omits the price does not push the obligation to a later message (FAQ Q23).
3. **Two clean auto-responder configurations** (FAQ Q23, Guide "Mind the automated first touch"): merge the vehicle's total price from inventory into the auto-reply itself, or limit the auto-reply to a bare administrative receipt ("we received your message") with no vehicle reference, no marketing content, and no credit invitation, and put the total price in the first substantive reply. Under the second configuration the receipt precedes the first response; it does not replace it.
4. **Text opt-in requests and confirmations are consent mechanics only** (FAQ Q24). Nothing beyond the consent language: no vehicle, no price, no promotion. The first substantive text after consent carries the total price.
5. **AI chat agents** (FAQ Q25): once the customer identifies a specific vehicle, the next substantive reply, from the agent or the human it hands to, states the total price. A bare transfer notice ("connecting you with our team") may precede it, and the price then follows promptly.
6. **State the price in the message body** (FAQ Q26). A link to the VDP is supplemental. The page can change after sending, so a retained link-only message will not show what was disclosed. Do not build a link-in-place-of-price template without counsel.
7. **Digital retailing messages count** (FAQ Q27). A salesperson's message through the DR tool satisfies the rule if the message itself displays the total price and the tool retains it two years. The internet-tool carve-out covers consumer-adjusted estimates only.
8. **Credit-only inquiries with no vehicle trigger nothing** (FAQ Q28). Do not quote rates, payments, or dollar amounts until a vehicle is identified.
9. **Walk-ins** (FAQ Q30 to Q32): the first worksheet, printed quote, or electronic message is the first written communication. Document it with a retained worksheet or quote, a CRM-sent text or email while the customer is in the store, or the Reynolds Total Price Disclosure form (LAWCA-TOTPRCDISC). No signature is required; the disclosure must be made, not acknowledged. If the customer declines to sign, note the refusal, keep the unsigned copy and the transmission record, and continue. Signed worksheets carry a risk of being argued as a contract, so the separate form or a CRM message is preferred. The form is evidence of compliance, not compliance: a BDC first email that omitted the price is not cured by a later signature.
10. **Personal phones** (FAQ Q33): only through a dealership-managed app that routes the message through the CRM or a retained platform; prohibit texting outside it. Labor Code § 2802 expense reimbursement applies when personal devices are used for work; put both in a written policy.
11. **Every shopper gets it** (FAQ Q34). The obligation runs to each consumer who receives a first written communication about the vehicle.
12. **Itemized first offers** (FAQ Q35): the total price appears as its own figure equal to the advertised price; any add-on is listed separately with the written statement that it is not required and the customer can buy or lease without it; any quoted payment carries the total of payments and assumed consideration. Prohibited: a single "your price" that folds add-ons into the vehicle figure, a "price with protection package", products without the optionality statement, pre-selected products the customer must remove, a payment quote that includes products without saying so. The simplest compliant first response states the total price and nothing more.
13. **Brokers are dealers** (FAQ Q36). Comply as if the broker message were to the consumer: state the total price in the first written response to the broker, give the broker a compliant total price notice to pass on, and write to the customer directly where you can.
14. **Out-of-state shoppers get the total price anyway** (FAQ Q37). Incentives the customer does not qualify for do not change the total price; disclose incentives separately with eligibility.
15. **Storage** (FAQ Q38, Q66): in the CRM, attached to the customer record and the stock number, searchable and exportable. Export the full two-year history before any CRM migration; the obligation is the dealer's regardless of vendor.
16. **Safest BDC reply** (Guide Appendix D Ex. 5): total price, no payment figure. A written payment quote during negotiation additionally requires the total amount payable at that payment and any assumed consideration (§ 1784.41(c)).
17. **Date it.** Put an expiration date in every CRM and BDC template that states a total price and honor any unexpired price; the advertised price is otherwise a ceiling for anyone while the unit is unsold (FAQ Q16, Q17; Veh. Code § 11713.1(e)).

## Two compliant template strategies (generate both, let the client pick)

**Strategy 1: Price-forward.** The autoresponder includes the vehicle and its total price via merge field. Cleanest compliance, best for stores with accurate feed pricing. Most CRMs can merge the price from inventory.

**Strategy 2: Bare receipt first touch.** The autoresponder confirms receipt ONLY: no vehicle reference, no dollar amount or term, no marketing line, no "apply for credit" link, no appointment pitch that names the unit. The price disclosure then rides on the first substantive reply that references the vehicle. Use when feed pricing is unreliable. Warn the client: every rep's first written reply becomes the compliance surface, so lock templates and train (see -training mode). CNCDA's word for the receipt is "administrative transmission"; anything more makes it the response.

## Required elements for a Strategy 1 template

1. Vehicle identified (year/make/model + VIN or stock #; VIN or license number is the Vehicle Code standard, stock number alone is not sufficient for advertising).
2. **Total price**, labeled "Total Price", in the body of the message, near the vehicle reference, same font prominence. Never link-only.
3. Exclusions statement, verbatim: "Plus government fees and taxes, any finance charges, any dealer document processing charge, any electronic filing charge, and any emission testing charge." If the store includes the doc fee in its price, drop that phrase from the sentence.
4. Optional federal presentation (CNCDA Appendix D Fig. D-5C): a line for "Document processing charge (not a governmental fee): $X" and "Price including document processing charge: $Y", both figures still excluding taxes and government fees, never labeled "out-the-door".
5. Expiration date for the stated price.
6. If any payment is mentioned (avoid it): total amount to be paid at that payment plus any assumed down payment or trade value, and the lower-payment notice if payments are compared.
7. No guarantee language (financing, availability), no "pre-approved", no add-on folded into the vehicle figure (CC 1784.40).
8. Retention configured (see below); an archive note in the footer is optional.

## Message shapes CNCDA published (Appendix D Example 5, adapted, fictitious figures)

Noncompliant first reply: "Great news, the 2023 Cascade you asked about is still available. With money down you'd be looking at around $350/month. When can you come in?" (quotes a financing amount, no total price).

Compliant first reply: "Great news, the 2023 Cascade you asked about (Stock #P4821, VIN ...KD240087) is still available. The vehicle's Total Price is $26,450. Plus government fees and taxes, any finance charges, any dealer document processing charge, any electronic filing charge, and any emission testing charge. Price valid through [date]. A range of financing options is available on approved credit; I'd be glad to go over real numbers at the store or by phone. When can you come in for a test drive?"

Federal-presentation variant: same message with "Total Price: $26,450 / Document processing charge (not a governmental fee): $85 / Price including document processing charge: $26,535. Both figures exclude government fees and taxes, which depend on your situation."

## Non-CRM first-touch surfaces (cover all of them or the CRM work is moot)

- Website chat (managed or AI): scripted and AI price answers must pull the total price on the next substantive reply after a unit is named; transcripts must sync to the CRM.
- SMS platforms outside the CRM: same rules; opt-in flows carry consent text only; personal-phone quoting is a retention violation waiting to happen, recommend a written no-personal-phone policy with a CRM-routed app (in -policy template).
- Digital retailing tool: dealer-initiated messages carry the total price and, if a payment is shown, the total of payments and assumed consideration; the tool's opening price must match the VDP.
- 3rd-party lead marketplaces that send templated replies on the dealer's behalf: audit their template once, archive proof, and confirm in writing that the vendor retains the message two years.
- Brokers: give them a compliant total price notice to pass to the customer.

## Deliverable format

Per client: one branded PDF containing Strategy 1 + Strategy 2 email and SMS versions (plus the SMS consent-only opt-in text and the AI-chat transfer notice), the CRM click-path to install them, and a one-page retention configuration checklist (CRM archive setting, inbound-plus-outbound retention, export test, migration export rule, personal-phone policy). Save editable copies to `Projects/{CODE}/cars-act-templates/` with a version date in the filename; Agent D archives them each run.
