## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy implements the California Combating Auto Retail Scams (CARS) Act, SB 766, Cal. Civ. Code § 1784.20 et seq., operative October 1, 2026. It applies to all retail vehicle sales and leases and to every employee and vendor who advertises vehicles, communicates prices, sells add-on products, or handles customer records for {DEALERSHIP NAME}. Consumer protections under the Act cannot be waived; no waiver form, including any vendor-supplied form, may be presented to a customer. Compliance lead: {ROLE}.

## 2. Advertising and pricing (CC 1784.41(a); Veh. Code 11713.1)

1. Every advertisement that references a specific vehicle, or states a monetary amount or financing term for a specific vehicle, clearly and conspicuously states the vehicle's total price. An advertisement is any statement we communicate to the public to promote a sale: the website ({DOMAIN}), every third-party listing fed from our inventory, the digital retailing tool, window stickers and addendums, paid social and search, email blasts, mailers, and social media posts, including posts by employees on personal accounts.
2. Total price includes every item installed on the vehicle at the time of the ad and any market adjustment, and is never reduced by a rebate. "Pay or remove" exclusions and "price excludes dealer-installed accessories" wording are prohibited. Rebates are shown under the total price as specific "Factory Rebate" amounts with the after-rebate figure labeled "Net Cost", never "Price"; no dealer cash back, no "up to" rebate ranges, no conflicting stacks.
3. "Call for Price", "Contact dealer for price", and any button or form that stands in place of the price are prohibited. MSRP may appear only labeled as MSRP and no more prominently than the total price; MSRP-only listings and "MSRP is not the selling price" disclaimers are prohibited.
4. Every specific vehicle is identified by model, model-year, and the distinguishing portion of the VIN or the license number. Every price display carries the statutory sentence verbatim: "Plus government fees and taxes, any finance charges, any dealer document processing charge, any electronic filing charge, and any emission testing charge." {IF DPC INCLUDED IN PRICE: the doc-fee phrase is removed from that sentence.} Every priced offer carries an expiration date, because the advertised price binds us for any customer while the unit is unsold.
5. One total price per vehicle across the website, the digital retailing tool, syndicated listings, program platforms (TrueCar, Costco), and the lot or showroom list. A lower figure anywhere becomes the advertised price for everyone. Program discounts appear separately under the same total price.
6. Advertised vehicles are available at the advertised price; sold or unavailable units are removed from every channel within 48 hours. Pricing errors are corrected immediately and the error and correction are recorded; upfitted units are pulled from feeds until repriced. Owner: {ROLE}.
7. Third-party listings are audited on a schedule and the audits retained. A discrepancy on a manufacturer-controlled or scraped listing is documented and a written correction request is sent; written notice to the manufacturer is kept because it triggers indemnity for manufacturer-preselected vendors (Veh. Code 11713.13(f)(1)(E)).
8. Addendums list installed equipment only, every item in the total price; no $0 optional-products addendum; no addendum on used vehicles; new units above MSRP itemize each added item and label remaining markup as added mark-up. The showroom price list covers all advertised inventory, is refreshed with the website, and drops sold units within 48 hours (Veh. Code 11709.3).
9. Federal posture: FTC staff (April 2026) expect the document processing charge inside the most prominent price. {DEALERSHIP NAME} {includes the DPC in the advertised price | uses the CNCDA dual presentation: Total Price, Document processing charge (not a governmental fee), Price including document processing charge, equal prominence, never labeled "out-the-door"}. Counsel reviews this election when the FTC issues written guidance.
10. Employees may not post vehicles, prices, or payments on any social media account without approval through {MARKETING ROLE}. Marketing vendors (including DigitalCLIQ) receive this policy and confirm template compliance in writing annually.

## 3. First written communication (CC 1784.41(a)(3))

1. Our first written communication with a consumer that references a specific vehicle, or any monetary amount or financing term, states that vehicle's total price at least once. A reply to a vehicle-specific inquiry is a response about that vehicle whether or not it names the vehicle; a generic acknowledgment or a credit-application invitation does not defer the duty.
2. Auto-replies in {CRM} either merge the vehicle's total price or are limited to a bare receipt with no vehicle reference, no marketing content, and no credit invitation, in which case the first substantive reply states the total price. SMS opt-in and consent messages contain consent language only.
3. {CHAT VENDOR / AI AGENT} states the total price on the next substantive reply after a customer names a specific vehicle, or sends only a transfer notice, with the human's first message stating the price promptly.
4. The total price is stated in the body of the message. A link to the vehicle page is supplemental, never a substitute.
5. Only approved templates installed in {CRM} may be used for first-touch responses. Free-form first replies that quote prices or payments are prohibited. Credit-only inquiries receive no rates, payments, or dollar amounts until a vehicle is identified. The default first reply states the total price and no payment figure.
6. Walk-ins: the first worksheet, printed quote, or CRM message referencing the vehicle carries the total price and is retained; the Total Price Disclosure form (LAWCA-TOTPRCDISC) is used where no prior written contact exists. Signatures are not required; a refusal is noted and the unsigned copy kept.
7. Personal cell phones may be used for customer communication only through {APPROVED CRM-ROUTED APP}; texting outside it is prohibited. The dealership reimburses business use of personal devices per Labor Code 2802.
8. Messages to brokers state the total price and brokers receive a compliant total price notice to pass to the customer. Out-of-state shoppers receive the total price as a matter of course.
9. Every first written communication is retained at least two years in {CRM}, attached to the customer record and stock number, searchable and exportable, inbound and outbound, and produced to the consumer on written request. Before any CRM migration the full two-year history is exported in searchable form. Owner: {ROLE}.

## 4. Worksheets, payment quotes, and comparisons (CC 1784.41(c), (d))

1. Every written monthly payment, on every pencil column and every digital retailing screen, is accompanied by the total the customer will pay at that payment and by any assumed down payment, trade value, or applied rebate. Lease columns show the total of scheduled lease payments.
2. Every written comparison that discusses lower monthly payments carries the statement that lower monthly payments often increase the total amount the customer will pay. {PAYMENT TOOL} is configured to display the total of payments and assumed consideration alongside any payment.
3. The Pre-Contract Disclosure (LAWCA-PCD 10/26) is completed on every retail installment sale, matches the contract exactly, and is regenerated whenever the deal changes. Disclosures are provided in Spanish, Chinese, Tagalog, Vietnamese, or Korean when the negotiation is primarily in that language, with the figures on the translated form; both versions are retained.
4. No employee may guarantee financing approval, state that a customer is pre-approved, or represent an application outcome before the lender decision.

## 5. Optional add-on products (CC 1784.41(b); CC 1784.42)

1. Every written add-on presentation (F&I menu, worksheet, digital retailing screen) states, clearly and in writing, that the add-on is not required and the customer may buy or lease the vehicle without it. The Optional Add-On Products or Services Disclosure (LAWCA-OPD) is signed at contracting on every deal, listing every add-on with a plain-English description and price; "protection package" is not an acceptable description.
2. No product is pre-selected or defaulted into a payment. Customer election is documented on the menu and the OPD.
3. Products installed on a vehicle before it is advertised are inside the total price. Hybrid products carry a real base warranty inside the price with upgraded coverage offered separately. Hardware is never described as "free" when its cost is recovered through a subscription, and no device is deactivated because a customer declines a subscription. Customer-ordered accessories installed after sale are due-bill items with the optionality disclosure.
4. Prohibited products are never sold, and {DMS / MENU} eligibility screens block them: nitrogen tire products below 95% purity, products providing no coverage, non-compliant GAP (loan-to-value calculated and retained on every GAP deal per Civ. Code 2982.12), service contracts void for preexisting conditions, oil changes on electric vehicles, catalytic converter marking on vehicles without converters, and surface protection that voids the factory paint warranty. Catalytic converter marking bundled with a theft-reimbursement benefit is not offered without the vendor's written California-law basis and counsel's concurrence.
5. Third-party add-on providers are paid within 10 days of contract signing or per a written agreement that does not affect customer coverage; proof of timely payment is retained. Owner: {ROLE}.
6. A benefit file is maintained per product (vendor description, terms, eligibility rules, OEM warranty compatibility, vendor's written classification). Product-level penetration rates are reviewed {QUARTERLY} with counsel.

## 6. Three-day right to cancel (CC 1784.43; CC 1784.31(g), (i))

1. Every retail sale or lease of a used vehicle at a purchase price or capitalized cost of $50,000 or less receives the free 3-day right to cancel, documented on the separate "3-Day Right to Cancel Used Car Purchase or Lease" disclosure (LAWCA-CANCELDSCL), signed the same date as the agreement, translated when required. Eligibility is not reduced by optional add-ons that raise the amount financed. The right does not apply to new vehicles, motorcycles, lease-end buyouts to the lessee in possession, or auction sales.
2. The period is three calendar days starting the day after the agreement is executed, ending at our posted close of business, extended to the next open day when day three falls on a day we are closed. The deadline entered on the disclosure is never earlier than that; {IF VOLUNTARY EXTENSION: we run the period from delivery and enter that deadline}. Odometer readings are recorded at execution and at delivery.
3. If exercised (vehicle returned during business hours by the deadline, driven 400 miles or less since execution, in delivered condition except reasonable wear and post-delivery defects not caused by the customer, free of liens, with all cash and items returned): restocking fee of 1.5% of the sale price (minimum $200, maximum $600, or actual shipping cost up to that cap where a shipping fee was charged) plus $1 per mile over 250 up to $150. The fee is deducted from the refund first; only a shortfall is collected from the customer. The contract is cancelled and the refund issued within 48 hours of exercise; for payments not immediately verifiable, within two business days after verification, with documentation to the customer. Unverified funds never justify refusing the cancellation. Refunds include the sales tax attributable to refunded amounts; registration fees are refunded and are not a deduction.
4. Trade-ins are held for the cancellation window where practicable. If a trade-in has been sold or entered title transfer, the customer is paid the greatest of the contract allowance, the gross amount we sold it for, or fair market value supported by a 7-day written offer or a retained pricing-guide printout, less only indebtedness we actually paid, with an itemized receipt and a redacted copy of the sale document.
5. A cancellation is a DMV rollback: the report of sale stands, transfer-back documents (bill of sale, REG 262, REG 256) are completed, the lender is notified and the assignment cancelled, and tax is reversed in the DMS and claimed with CDTFA. Owner: {ROLE}.
6. No employee may impede exercise of the right, demand the fee in cash, claim excess wear without a documented, photographed, reasonable basis (a service diagnosis is obtained where causation is unclear), or state that the person authorized to return funds or trade-ins is unavailable. A Notice of Failure to Meet Cancellation Requirement(s) (LAWCA-CANCELFAIL) is issued only on specific, supported grounds, reviewed and signed by {MANAGER ROLE}; a customer who cures within the period is processed.
7. All "no cooling-off period" signage, the repealed two-day contract cancellation option form, and any web or contract copy describing a purchased option, a 72-hour option, or a $40,000 threshold are removed. The 36-point physical notice is posted in every sales office and cubicle where written terms are discussed and in every contract room, with the motorcycle/off-highway supplement where applicable. The first-page contract language appears on every purchase and lease agreement (Reynolds RISC 553CA1026; leasing-company forms confirmed). Owner: {ROLE}.
8. Any more generous return terms we offer are written into the cancellation disclosure and the signage and applied consistently.

## 7. Recordkeeping (CC 1784.44; CC 1784.41(a)(3)(B))

Retained at least two years from creation, audit-ready: records showing our advertised and communicated total prices complied (the DMS or inventory-provider feed export by vehicle and date, with the vendor's written confirmation of two-year retrievability, plus DigitalCLIQ's dated listing and third-party captures archived to the dealership's Google Drive compliance folder; creative with run dates and placement where it states a price or payment for a specific vehicle; mailers with mailing date and list identifiers); every first written communication; signed purchase orders and finance/lease documents including unfinalized deals; all written sales, financing, and leasing communications, including every worksheet, for customers who sign; add-on benefit files, GAP loan-to-value calculations, and proof of vendor payment; cancellation requests, refund proof, and trade-in return proof; and written customer complaints and written inquiries and responses about advertised vehicles (surveys, reviews, and testimonials excluded). Records may be kept in any legible form; wet-signed originals are kept 90 days before digitizing. Longer DMV and ASFA periods still apply to their documents. Records custodian: {ROLE}.
