## 1. Visual Identity

**Rule 1a: Brand Symbol Usage**
- Every ad MUST include exactly ONE Nissan Brand Symbol (Primary Logo or Wordmark)
- Cannot use more than one Brand Symbol in a single ad
- Exception: Dealer websites may use the Brand Symbol throughout multiple pages
- Minimum size in print: at least **1 cm wide**

**Rule 1b: Digital & Social Standards**
- Dealer social media platforms must remain **solely Nissan specific** (no other OEM brands)
- All digital media must be **high resolution** and **easily legible**: internet banners, third-party websites, mass emails, web pages, specials, sliders on dealer websites

---

## 2. Brand Symbol Rules

**Rule 2a: Logo Integrity**
- Available in **black or white only** (with or without shadow)
- Must **never be altered**: not rotated, stretched, animated, outlined, filtered, or given special effects
- Must be downloaded from **www.nissanartwork.com**

**Rule 2b: Clear Space & Placement**
- Must maintain **sufficient clear space** from other elements
- Permitted placements: top right, top left, bottom right, bottom left, centered at top, centered at bottom
- White logo on red background ("Nissan Primary/Social Logo") is **only for social media**

---

## 3. Typography & Spelling

**Rule 3a**: No misspellings of "Nissan" or any model names

**Rule 3b: Font Requirements**
- "Nissan," all model names, and trims must use **Nissan Brand fonts** (from nissanartwork.com)
- Acceptable alternatives: **Verdana, Arial, or Helvetica** (traditional forms)
- All mentions of "Nissan," models, and trims: **black, grey, or white text only**

---

## 4. Clear, Positive, Brand-Enhancing Communication

**Rule 4a: Prohibited Content**
- No misleading advertising (inaccurate model years)
- No sexual, religious, racial, political, or violent content
- No criticizing competitors
- No crude or slapstick humor
- No AI-generated imagery that misrepresents Nissan vehicles or brand elements

**Rule 4b: Dealership Identification**
- Must use official **Nissan DBA** name and contact info
- Logo lockups require full DBA name

**Rule 4c: National/Regional TV Campaign Tags**
- Only tags from **nissanartwork.com**, unaltered
- Dealership DBA and contact info in designated time slot at end
- No adding graphics on top of or copying over Nissan mnemonic tag

---

## 5. Pricing Rules

**Rule 5a: VIN Requirements**
- Specific price offers on new current MY vehicles must include **last 8 or full 17-digit VIN** next to each offer (NOT in disclaimer)
- Exception: radio broadcast
- 3+ prices/models: last 8 or full 17 of **3 VINs** required
- Paid search, dealer websites, social, third-party sites: VINs can be **"one click away"**

**Rule 5b: Special/Conditional Offers**
- College grad, military, VPP, first responder, etc. must clearly state **"must qualify"** or **"conditional"**
- Exception: radio broadcast disclaimers
- Conditional incentives may NOT be used to reduce selling price below MAP

**Rule 5c: Lease Offer Display**
- Down payment or due at signing must be shown **next to the offer** (not only in disclaimer)
- Exception: radio broadcast
- All calculations must be mathematically correct
- Selling price used in lease calculation must be MAP compliant (MY25+)

**Rule 5d: Incentive Language**
- Approved terms: "Incentive," "Rebate," "Customer cash," "Cash back," "Bonus cash," "Customer bonus cash," "Cash voucher"
- Approved sources: Nissan, NMAC, full Region names, Dealer DBA, Factory, Manufacturer

---

## 6. Prohibited Words & Phrases

**Rule 6b: ALWAYS Prohibited:**

| Prohibited | Also Prohibited |
|---|---|
| "liquidate" | "prices too low to advertise" |
| "blowout" | "overstock" |
| "distress" | "wholesale" (pricing context) |
| "bailout" | "invoice" (pricing context) |
| "we will not be undersold" | "dealer cost" / "employee cost" / "our cost" |
| "guaranteed lowest price" | Any employee pricing implication |
| "lowest price guaranteed" | "inventory reduction" / "disposal" |

**Prohibited Price-Comparison Words:**
- "meet," "beat," "match," "unbeatable," "deal beater," "surpass," "crush" (in price reference context)

**Prohibited EXCEPT During Official Nissan Sales Events:**
- "sell off," "sell down," "clearance," "markdown"

## 7. Pricing Restrictions

**Rule 6c: Discount Language**
Must NOT include dollar/percentage discounts unless itemized from MSRP. Prohibited phrasing includes:
- "$XXXX savings," "$XXXX discount," "discount/savings up to $XXXX"
- "save $XXXX," "$XXXX in savings," "$XXXX off"
- "save thousands," "thousands off," "thousands in savings"
- "$XXXX voucher," "$XXXX coupon," "$XXXX towards your purchase," "$XXXX bonus check"
- "% off," "save %," "% in savings"

**Rule 6d: MSRP Accuracy**
- Advertised MSRP cannot exceed **true MSRP** (Monroney label)
- Price above actual MSRP (due to dealer accessories) **cannot be labeled "MSRP"**

---

## 8. Brand Exclusivity & New vs Used

**Rule 7a: 50% New Vehicle Requirement**
- At least **50%** of vehicle-specific ad space must be new Nissan vehicles
- No more than 50% may be used vehicles
- New space: headline/sub-headline, special events, sales elements, vehicles, price offers
- Used space: used car headline, Nissan CPO, Security+Plus, mixed used
- Excluded from calculation: dealership name, map, hours, address, phone, dealership photography
- Multi-category ads: sections clearly labeled, separated by **solid box/line (min 1pt)** full width/height
- **Broadcast must be exclusively Nissan**

**Rule 7b: New vs Used Designation**
- Mention of "new" = new car ad
- Recent MY presumed new unless labeled: "used," "pre-driven," "previously registered," "rental," "pre-owned"

## 9. Nissan Events & Claims

**Rule 8a: Official Sales Events**
- Use assets/logos from **nissanartwork.com** only, no structural alteration
- Standard text can switch between black or white
- Only during **NNA-approved run dates**
- After event ends: **remove ALL logos/mentions immediately** from all advertising including websites

**Rule 8b: Claims & Accolades**
- Sales claims must specify: **parameters** (new car sales, largest volume), **time frame** (current, within one calendar year), **source** (Nissan sales report)
- No Consumer Reports references
- No directly contacting companies for verification

**Rule 8c: Awards**
- Only most recent Nissan Owner First Award of Excellence and Nissan Global Award
- Award logos must not be altered or distorted

## 12. Minimum Advertised Price (MAP): BAP Guidelines

### MAP Calculation
```
MAP = Invoice Price (incl. D&H)
      - Applicable Consumer Incentives (available to ALL customers)
      - Variable Invoice Assessments (Marketing Assessment & Warranty Supplemental Charge)
      ... calculated per VIN

Maximum Total Savings = Nissan MSRP - MAP
```

### Applicability
- Applies to **MY25 and forward**
- **ARIYA is included** in MAP policy
- LEAF is **excluded** from MAP
- Pre-MY25 vehicles are **excluded** from MAP
- Hail-damaged vehicles: may offer below MAP with **supporting documentation**

### Rule B3A: VIN Requirements (MAP)
- Same as Rule 5a: last 8 or full 17 VIN next to offer
- 3+ models: 3 VINs required
- Digital: VINs can be "one click away"

### Rule B3B: Retail Price & Savings
- Must NOT advertise below MAP
- Cannot inflate MSRP to imply greater savings
- Total savings cannot exceed MSRP minus MAP
- Cannot reduce by conditional incentives (college grad, military, loyalty, conquest, first responder, tax credits)
- Accessories: discount ≤ max savings + accessories cost (excl. labor)
- Selling price must be MAP compliant, shown next to monthly payment or in disclaimer (digital: "one click away")

### Rule B3C: Lease Payment (MAP)
- Selling price used for lease calculation must NOT be below MAP
- Selling price must appear next to offer or in disclaimer (digital: "one click away")

### Rule B3D: Finance Payment (MAP)
- Selling price used for finance/APR calculation must NOT be below MAP
- Finance offers must disclose selling price, APR, term, and total cost

### Accessories & MAP
- Dealer-installed accessories added to a vehicle can increase advertised price above MSRP, but the base vehicle price cannot be labeled "MSRP" if it includes accessories
- Discount on accessories: limited to max savings amount + cost of accessories (excluding labor)

## 13. RMP Co-Marketing Fund Structure

### Fund Composition
| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Nissan contribution** | Per-vehicle amount credited at wholesale |
| **Dealer contribution** | Matching amount deducted at invoice |
| **Total fund** | Combined Nissan + dealer contributions |

### Fund Usage
- Funds must be used within the **program year** (typically calendar year)
- **Unused funds forfeit** at year-end
- Rebates/discounts from media outlets must be shared with the Co-Op Program

### Certified Digital Providers
- Certain digital media categories require use of **Nissan-certified providers** for co-op eligibility
- Certified categories include: SEM/PPC, SEO, Social Media Management, Website, Digital Retailing, Reputation Management
- Non-certified digital providers may be eligible for co-op but at reduced or no reimbursement depending on category

### Eligible Media
**Digital:** Websites, digital retailing, SEM/PPC, SEO, social media (organic + paid), display/banner ads, OLV (online video), CTV/OTT, streaming audio, email marketing, conquest/equity mining, third-party listing sites, Google PMAX, online chat

**Traditional:** TV, radio, streaming radio, newspaper, magazine, direct mail, outdoor/billboards, cinema, transit

**Events:** Sponsorships, community events, auto shows (with prior approval)

**CRM:** Direct mail, eNewsletters, SmartLeads, concierge services

### Ineligible Expenses
- Agency fees for non-certified providers (in certified-required categories)
- Reputation management (standalone)
- Giveaways, prizes, alcohol, talent fees, event staffing
- Taxes and regulatory fees
- Any media containing non-compliant content

## 14. Infraction Policy

### Escalation Schedule
| Strike | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| 1st | Warning + DOM consultation |
| 2nd | Warning + DOM consultation |
| 3rd | **60-day suspension** from RMP |
| 4th | Additional **30-day suspension** |
| 5th | **Six-month suspension** from RMP |

### Process
- Violation identified → dealer notified
- Follow-up review at **Day 7**
- Correction required by **Day 14** or Strike is issued
- Clear record: **3 consecutive months** of full compliance
- Appeals: within **30 days** of Strike letter

### BAP-Specific Infractions
- MAP violations tracked separately from RMP
- Repeated MAP violations may result in additional penalties including loss of BAP fund eligibility
- BAP infractions follow same escalation timeline

## Quick Compliance Checklist

### Logo & Identity
- [ ] Exactly ONE Brand Symbol (logo or wordmark) present
- [ ] Brand Symbol at least 1 cm wide (print), high-res (digital)
- [ ] Brand Symbol is black or white only, unaltered, from nissanartwork.com
- [ ] Brand Symbol has sufficient clear space and correct placement

### Typography & Messaging
- [ ] "Nissan," model names, and trims spelled correctly in approved fonts
- [ ] Text in black, grey, or white only
- [ ] No prohibited content (sexual, religious, racial, political, violent, competitor-bashing, crude humor)
- [ ] Official Nissan DBA and contact info used
- [ ] No prohibited words or phrases (see Section 6)

### Pricing & Offers
- [ ] VINs next to specific price offers (or one click away for digital)
- [ ] Special/conditional offers clearly marked ("must qualify" / "conditional")
- [ ] Lease down payment/due at signing shown next to offer
- [ ] Incentive language uses approved terms and sources
- [ ] No unapproved discount/savings language
- [ ] MSRP not inflated above Monroney label

### MAP Compliance (MY25+)
- [ ] No advertising below MAP
- [ ] Selling price is MAP compliant
- [ ] Conditional incentives not used to reduce below MAP
- [ ] Lease/finance selling price not below MAP

### Content Mix & Exclusivity
- [ ] At least 50% of vehicle ad space is new Nissan
- [ ] Multi-category ads have clear labels and 1pt+ dividing lines
- [ ] Broadcast is exclusively Nissan
- [ ] Social media is solely Nissan specific
- [ ] Homepage does not reference other OEMs

### Events & Website
- [ ] Sales event assets from nissanartwork.com, within approved dates, removed after event
- [ ] Sales claims specify parameters, time frame, and source
- [ ] Website: no broken links, has inventory search, links to/from NissanUSA.com
