## 2. Logo Requirements

### General Rules (All Brands)
- **Original form only**: no modifications to Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, or Fiat logos
- **No alterations:** color, proportions, drop shadows, outlines, glows, stretching, compression
- **Only place logos on** monochrome or image backgrounds with proper contrast
- **Clear space / isolation area** required around every logo
- **Do not embed logos in text**: type out brand names instead of using logo as inline text
- **Source:** Only approved logos from the **CARGO website**
- **2D is preferred Ram logo**

### Brand-Specific Rules
- **Ram is a standalone brand**: NEVER use "Dodge Ram" or "Dodge Ram 1500"
- National/regional campaign logos are **mandatory** during active promotion periods when required by Stellantis
- Old campaign logos or mentions are **prohibited** from ads

## 3. Website Compliance

> Items marked **[CO-OP]** are required for co-op reimbursement eligibility.

### Homepage
| Requirement | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Search bar and/or model selector prominently above the fold | **[CO-OP]** |
| Only Stellantis brands on homepage (new, CPO, used) | **[CO-OP]** |
| Max ONE pop-up per site; doesn't reopen after close | **[CO-OP]** |
| Color scheme follows Tier 1 brand guidelines | Required |
| Mobile: Click-to-Call and Directions always visible | **[CO-OP]** |

### Navigation
| Requirement | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Utility bar + main nav: contrasting **black and white only** | **[CO-OP]** |
| Utility bar (contact/address) above main nav bar | Required |
| Dealer logo left-justified in nav, links to homepage | Required |
| Brand logo(s) in nav bar; **no competitive brand logos** | Required |
| Navigation in one row (no wrapping) | Required |
| Max 8 menu tabs recommended | Recommended |
| Required tab order: 1. New, 2. Pre-Owned | Required |
| Recommended: 3. Specials, 4. Finance, 5. Service & Parts, 6. About | Recommended |

### Inventory (VLPs & VDPs)
| Requirement | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Only new Stellantis makes on new inventory pages | **[CO-OP]** |
| Competitive used vehicles: only on used listing pages, NOT homepage | Required |
| New inventory shared within 100-mile radius; show vehicle location | Required |
| Used inventory shared across locations without restriction | Allowed |
| MSRP displayed on VLP and VDP, labeled "MSRP" (from daily Stellantis file) | **[CO-OP]** |
| Max 5 CTAs on VLP and VDP | **[CO-OP]** |
| CTA colors align with brand palette | Required |
| eVN In-Transit images required | **[CO-OP]** |

### Call Tracking
| Requirement | Status |
|------------|--------|
| Stellantis Digital-provisioned numbers in header on all pages | **[CO-OP]** |
| Numbers across site match Stellantis-provisioned numbers | Required |
| All in-program digital ads point to Stellantis-provisioned numbers | Required |

### Third-Party Tools
- All links must stay within dealer site environment
- Exceptions: social media, approved Stellantis sites, charity/local events
- Non-certified lead tools: remove within **10 business days** of notification
- If not removed, Stellantis Digital Support may remove directly

### Program Domains
- Site URL must be program URL of record, **no redirects** to dealer group/retail/secondary sites **[CO-OP]**
- No site launches in last 5 business days of month without written permission
- No launches on Fri/Sat/Sun/holidays without written permission
- Stellantis Digital must own registration when only Stellantis trademark or geo + trademark used
- All leads route through **Stellantis Lead Management System (ELMS)**

## 4. DAP Advertising Content Rules

### Content Requirements
| Rule | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| **1A** | 100% of ad must support CDJR/FIAT vehicles. Must state current/previous MY or "New." |
| **1B** | No combined ads with any other manufacturer |
| **2A** | At least **75%** new CDJR/FIAT; up to **25%** CPOV. Standalone CPOV ads ineligible |
| **2B** | New vehicle section clearly separated from CPOV by solid box/line. CPOV section must include CPOV logo |
| **2C** | Up to **10%** parts and service. Standalone parts/service ads ineligible |
| **5A** | Must include all applicable FCA US LLC brand logos |
| **5B** | National/regional campaign logos **mandatory** during active promotions |
| **5C** | Ram is standalone brand: never "Dodge Ram" |
| **5D** | Only approved logos from CARGO website. No distortion/alteration |
| **6** | **No traditional used/pre-owned vehicles or mention.** Any reference makes entire ad ineligible |
| **10C** | Vehicle listing images: clean vehicle, clean environment, vehicle is hero |

## 5. Prohibited Messaging (Rule 7)

**Distressed messaging: entire ad becomes ineligible:**
- Bailout
- Blowout
- Liquidation
- Wholesale
- Distress / Factory Outlet
- Factory Authorized
- Manufacturer/Factory Challenged
- Below Invoice / Under Invoice
- Buy One, Get One
- Any reference to recall notices, safety issues, or technical service bulletins

---

## 6. Brand Communication Standards

**Rule 8: Competitive Positioning**
- Must NOT state or imply favorable status vs. other CDJR dealers
- No unverified claims of "#1," "preferred," "factory-selected," etc.

**Rule 9: Brand Enhancement**
All advertising must enhance the brand. Prohibited:
- Misleading or deceptive claims
- Religious, racial, or political overtones
- Insulting or profane language
- Discrimination or harassment

## 7. DAP Co-Op Fund Structure

### Fund Breakdown
| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Dealer contribution | $125 at vehicle invoice |
| Stellantis match | $275 at NVDR |
| **DAP Base Fund (75%)** | All eligible media, reimbursed 100% |
| **DAP Bonus Fund (25%)** | Certified Digital providers only: VIN-specific, OLV, CTV, PMAX, Social |

### Deadlines
| Action | Deadline |
|--------|----------|
| Spend period | Within 3 months of accrual month |
| Claim submission | Within 2 months after month ad ran |
| Missing documentation | Within 30 days of notification |
| Appeals | Within 30 days of denial notification |
| Pre-approval | tier3adcoop.com |

## 8. Eligible & Ineligible Media

### Certified Digital (auto-submitted)
SEM, Shopping Ads, Discovery Ads, Multi-Channel, PMAX, Display, OLV, CTV, OTT, SEO, Social, Email Marketing, Website Fees, Lead Generators, Equity Mining, Creative Services (up to $500/mo), Lead Reanimation, Third-Party Leads

### Non-Certified Digital (manual claim)
Display, OLV, OTT, SEO, Paid Social

### Traditional
TV, Cinema, Radio, Streaming Radio, Newspapers/Magazines, Direct Mail, Outdoor/Transit, Events

### Ineligible Expenses
- Agency fees for non-certified providers
- Non-certified SEM, Shopping Ads, PMAX, Creative Services, Equity Mining, Lead Reanimation, Lead Generators
- **Reputation Management** (not eligible via DAP regardless)
- Event staffing, alcohol at test drive events, giveaways, talent fees, taxes
