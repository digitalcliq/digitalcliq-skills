## 1. Corporate Identity & Typography

### Font Family
- **Primary font:** BMW TypeNextPro Medium (primary weight)
- **Available weights:** BMWTypeNextPro Light, Regular, Medium (primary), Bold
- **Body copy:** BMW TypeNextPro Light or Regular
- **Clickable elements:** BMW TypeNextPro Bold (always, in digital media)
- **Headlines and subheads:** UPPER CASE
- **Body copy:** Sentence case
- **Offer copy:** Sentence case
- **CTA buttons:** Title Case (e.g., View Offer, Learn More, Explore Now)
- **BMW M content:** Headlines and claim appear in BMW Type Next Bold

### Font Color & Background
- On dark/colored backgrounds: White text (matching white logo)
- On white backgrounds: 70% black text (matching logo line color)
- Body copy on white: 70% to 100% black permitted
- BMW Grey for text copy: #221F1F
- **M Brand exception:** White text on black backgrounds is permitted

### Non-BMW Font Exceptions
- Center logo may use a non-BMW font if that is the established logo font
- Helvetica, Arial, and other sans-serif fonts may be used for body copy, intermediate headlines, or price labelling
- Center taglines must still use BMW Type Next font

### Headline Rules
- Headlines and subheads: max two lines each
- Must end with a period (full stop), UNLESS it is a vehicle name used alone as headline (e.g., "BMW 3", "BMW X7", no period)
- When vehicle name is part of a phrase/sentence, period IS required (e.g., "VISIT AND TEST-DRIVE THE BMW 3 SERIES.")
- Numbers 1-12 should be written out (e.g., "three" not "3")
- Copy should NOT cover or obscure the vehicle

## 3. BMW Roundel Logo

### Format
- **Must use the 2D roundel**: the old 3D roundel is prohibited (outdated content)
- **Primary logo:** White + blue roundel, used against black, picture, or color backgrounds
- **Fallback logo:** Gray roundel, exceptional cases only, against white backgrounds
- BMW has only ONE logo: white and blue

### Six BMW CI Logo Principles
1. BMW has only one logo: white & blue
2. Clear memory hook: "blue / middle / white". "Upper right always bright"
3. The background needs to be controlled (color and brightness)
4. Don't touch the logo. Don't play with the logo
5. The pairing of core and subbrand is always mandatory, BMW is dominant
6. Any special cases to be approved

### Brand Claim / Tagline
- "The Ultimate Driving Machine" with ® symbol must be included with the BMW logo on most creative assets
- ® symbol should always follow "Machine" in the logo lock-up
- Brand claim is MANDATORY and must be placed under the logo within the type area
- "Ultimate" should NOT be bolded when used with the logo or as body copy
- Logo with claim should be used in most cases; only remove if it doesn't fit (small formats)

### Placement
- Logo with claim: **bottom left** or **bottom right** corner only
- BMW roundel in the **upper left corner** of the dealer website
- Must appear **prominently on every page**
- The dealership logo **cannot be more prominent** than the BMW roundel

### Sub-Brand Logos
- Sub-brand logos (M) must be placed to the **right** of the BMW roundel
- BMW + sub-brand pairing is always mandatory; BMW must be dominant
- BMW M logo pairing is accompanied by the BMW M claim

### Logo Background
- Roundel must be on a clean, non-distracting background
- Add gradients as needed when placed over pictures
- BMW blue must NOT be used as a full-surface background behind the logo
- Background must be controlled for color and brightness (see color matrix)

### Logo Spacing
- Minimum clear space: 6 units on all sides (roundel = 12x12 unit grid)

### Prohibited Uses
- Never recreate, modify, distort, or play with the roundel
- Never recreate outside advertising contexts
- **The BMW "i" sub-brand logo is NO LONGER used** (deprecated March 2026)

## 4. Vehicle Naming & Nomenclature

### Vehicle Name Rules
- Always prefix with "BMW": "BMW X5", "BMW 3". NOT "The X5" or "The 3"
- Do NOT use "The" before vehicle names (e.g., "BMW iX3" NOT "The BMW iX3")
- Exception: "The new BMW [model]" format is permitted for launches

### Nomenclature Styling

| Context | Example | Rule |
|---------|---------|------|
| Headlines | BMW 3 SERIES | UPPER CASE |
| Headlines | BMW X3 30 xDRIVE | xDRIVE stays lowercase x |
| Headlines | BMW CONNECTED DRIVE | Spaces between words |
| Body copy | BMW 3 Series | Sentence case |
| Body copy | BMW X3 30 xDrive | xDrive stays lowercase x |
| Body copy | BMW ConnectedDrive | CamelCase (no spaces) |
| Body copy | BMW ChargeForward | CamelCase |
| Body copy | BMW ChargingNetwork | CamelCase |
| Both | myBMW | Always lowercase "m" |
| Both | xDrive | Always lowercase "x" |
| Headlines | BMW M PERFORMANCE PARTS | UPPER CASE |
| Body copy | BMW M Performance Parts | Title Case |

### "New" Usage
- "New" in headlines: ONLY for vehicle launch campaigns, up to 6 months after market launch
- "New" in body: OK anytime for new vs. pre-owned distinction (VLPs, social)
- **"All New" is NEVER allowed**
- "First Ever": Only for first worldwide launch, up to 6 months, following corporate guidance
- Note: An LCI (Life Cycle Impulse/facelift) is NOT considered a product launch

## 5. Prohibited Content

### Imagery & Design
- No ancillary graphics, cartoons, or animations
- No excessive font sizes
- No damaged vehicles (exception: exemplary images on parts/service pages only)
- Copy/text must NOT cover or obscure the vehicle

### Messaging & Claims
- No distressed messaging: heavily discounted/below-invoice language
- No unsubstantiated claims ("Preferred Dealer," "#1 Dealer," "Recommended Dealer") unless objectively verifiable
- "All-new" and "new" wording only within 6 months of product launch (LCI excluded)

### Language to Avoid
- Speed/fast language: "pedal to the metal", "burn rubber", "eat dust", focus on power and performance instead
- No alcohol mentioned or insinuated
- Anything with "smoke"
- "Cheap", "discount", "deal", "affordable"
- Claims of "green", "free", "safe"
- Use "vehicle" instead of "car"
- Reserve "luxury" / "luxurious" for GKL creative only

### December Creative
- "Happy holidays" is OK
- Avoid: Santa Claus, Christmas trees, nutcrackers, or "Christmas" written anywhere

### Pricing in Headlines
- Lease price, APR, credits, and MSRP may **not** appear in the headline
- **Exceptions:** FMAs, banner ads, Paid Search, Paid Social

### Competitive References
- No advertising alongside other manufacturers
- No other brand or line make permitted, including website addresses
- No group logos (dealer group affiliation may be referenced once, in BMW font)

## 6. EV Messaging Guidelines (March 2026 Update)

**CRITICAL CHANGES:**
- **The BMW "i" logo is NO LONGER used**: deprecated in all communications
- **Do NOT use "100% electric" or "all-electric" in headlines**
- "Fully electric" or "all-electric" may ONLY be called out in body copy (not headlines)
- BMW i models (iX, i4, i5, i7, iX1, iX3) follow standard BMW naming without separate i logo
- Charging and range claims require proper disclaimers

## 7. BMW Certified (Formerly CPO)

### Program Branding
- Now branded as **"BMW Certified"** (updated branding)
- Primary logo: BMW roundel + "BMW CERTIFIED" text (white on dark background)
- Fallback logo: Gray roundel + gray "BMW CERTIFIED" text (on white background)
- Must only promote the BMW NA OEM Certified program, no third-party programs

### Inventory Requirements
- Content split: 70% BMW Certified / 30% non-certified BMW allowed
- All advertised vehicles must include VIN and/or stock number
- Must include dealer URL or bmwusa.com link

## 8. Lease & Finance Advertising

### Lease Terms
- **Term:** 36 months only
- **Mileage:** 10,000 miles/year (or 7,500 if offered by BMW FS in market)
- Center contribution + customer down payment: ≤ 10% of MSRP
- Combined customer cash + center contribution: ≤ 15% of MSRP
- Only credits available to ALL customers may be applied
- Advertised payment cannot be lower than National/MACO payment

### Sales Offer Requirements
New vehicle ads must include one of:
1. Lease payment, finance message, or "starting at" MSRP
2. Generic: "Special lease and finance offers available through BMW Financial Services"
3. For short radio: "Exceptional offers through BMW Financial Services"

## 9. Website Requirements

### Logo & Branding
- BMW Roundel in **upper left corner on every page**
- Dealership logo cannot be more prominent than the BMW roundel
- Must use current 2D roundel (not old 3D)

### URL Rules
- URL must be the dealer DBA name
- No reference to other franchises in URL
- One URL only per center
- Must link to BMW-exclusive website

### Content Requirements
- **50% or more** of dealer-developed creative must highlight BMW products, features, and current offers

### Web Accessibility (WCAG Level AA)
- All non-text content must have text alternatives (alt text)
- Text contrast ratio minimum 4.5:1 (large text 3:1)
- Headings and labels must describe topic or purpose
- Audio description for prerecorded video content

---

## 10. Social Media & Digital

- Use logo without brand claim in small formats
- If logo is in profile picture, doesn't need to appear on every frame. OK in end card only
- Default layout rules apply to social assets
- All digital advertising must use **radius targeting** per assigned Comparison Group
- TikTok & Instagram Reels guidelines apply

---

## 11. Center DBA & Branding

- Must advertise using DBA name, including dealer logos
- Dealer group affiliation: referenced **only once**, in BMW font, no group logos
- Center of Excellence: must include current logo, year awarded, consecutive years only
- BMW trademarks: Roundel, M logo, BMW word marks, model numbers, "The Ultimate Driving Machine", Motorsport colors, kidney-shaped grills

---

## 12. Co-Op Reimbursement

- Administered by **Papirfly** (formerly BrandMaster)
- Dealers choose 50% or 75% reimbursement rate
- Submit creative for **prior review before placement**
- Funds earned on wholesale purchases, must be used within calendar year

---

## 13. Violations & Enforcement

- Rolling 24-month tracking period
- 1st-3rd violations: Warning letters
- 4th violation: Co-Op budget reduced 25% OR 1% AVP bonus forfeited for 3 months
- Each subsequent: Additional 3-month forfeiture
