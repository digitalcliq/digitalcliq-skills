## 1. The Bowtie Logo

### Primary mark
- **3D gold bowtie** is the primary logo across nearly every touchpoint
- Lead with it whenever possible
- 5 approved lockup variants:
  1. Bowtie only
  2. Horizontal Brand Mark (bowtie + CHEVROLET wordmark)
  3. Vertical Brand Mark
  4. Horizontal TLD Lock Up (with "Together let's drive")
  5. Vertical TLD Lock Up

### Outlined (2D) bowtie
- Secondary mark, used only when production constraints or legibility issues prevent the 3D version
- Allowed over white, black, or rare secondary drive colors only
- Always try to lead with the primary 3D logo

### Clear space + minimum sizes
- Clear space = height of the bowtie wing (unit "X") on all sides
- Horizontal brand mark minimum: 2 in / 5 cm / 150 px
- Vertical brand mark minimum: 1 in / 2.5 cm / 78 px
- Logo must never appear below these minimums

### Logo violations (do not)
- Place the logo over backgrounds where legibility drops
- Create new lockups not on the approved list
- Alter colors of the bowtie or wordmark
- Rotate the bowtie
- Outline or add effects (drop shadows, glows, gradients on top)
- Alter the typeface or style of the wordmark

### Legibility over backgrounds
- Over photos: use darkening/lightening gradients to ensure contrast
- Over color backgrounds: ensure full contrast of the brand mark
- The bowtie must always be clearly visible

## 2. Tagline and TLD lockup

- Current brand tagline: **"Together let's drive"** (TLD), with trademark symbol
- Used in Print, Film, OOH, and CRM contexts
- Always use English ("Together let's drive") in all end tags
- Padding between wordmark and TLD tagline = half the height of the bowtie wing
- "And Chevrolet" platform: separate campaign line, follows the same lockup rules

### Co-branding lockups
- Optically balance the Chevrolet brand mark with partner / dealer mark
- Qualifier taglines set in Chevy Sans Narrow Medium
- Must follow approved co-branding lockup formats

## 4. Typography: Chevy Sans

### Font family
- **Chevy Sans** is the exclusive, custom-built typeface for Chevrolet
- Three families: Standard, Condensed, Narrow
- Weights in each family: Black through Thin
- **Chevy Sans Black** is the primary headline weight across Brand, Multiline, Truck, and SUV work

### Headline rules
- Set in **ALL CAPS**, Chevy Sans Black
- Large format (OOH, film, print): 95% cap-height leading, -20 tracking
- Small format (social, digital, web): 100% cap-height leading, -20 tracking

### Subhead rules
- Chevy Sans Medium, ALL CAPS, 90% cap-height leading, -20 tracking

### Body copy
- Chevy Sans Standard or Condensed (per brand guide), sentence case for long-form

## 5. Vehicle Nomenclature

### Brand naming
- Full brand: **CHEVROLET** in headlines and formal contexts
- Conversational/body: **Chevy** is acceptable
- Never: chevrolet (lowercase brand), Chev, Cheve

### Model naming
- Full model names: e.g., CHEVROLET SILVERADO, CHEVROLET TAHOE, CHEVROLET EQUINOX EV
- Conversational: Silverado, Tahoe, Equinox EV
- EV naming: EQUINOX EV, BLAZER EV, SILVERADO EV. "EV" follows the model name with a space
- Never invent or shorten model names

### Year designation
- "All New 2026 Silverado": only within launch window
- "New" usage rules per 2025 brand guide; verify before using outside a launch context

## 6. Photography & Imagery Direction

### Truck imagery
- Heritage of capability and toughness
- Outdoor / job-site / lifestyle scenes
- Vehicle must be hero; avoid clutter

### SUV imagery
- Family / adventure / lifestyle
- More aspirational than truck imagery, but never feels staged

### EV imagery
- Modern, clean, well-lit
- Charging integration optional but must look natural

### Photography do-nots
- No damaged vehicles
- No dirty / unkempt vehicles in standard creative (except deliberate off-road editorial)
- No copy or graphic elements covering the vehicle silhouette
- Never composite a vehicle into a scene where it could not realistically exist

## 7. Pricing & Disclaimer Type Specs

### GM legal dwell-time rule
- Disclaimers in video must hold on screen for **5 to 7 seconds minimum**
- Dwell time scales with disclaimer length
- Cannot rush disclaimer through; legibility is a hard requirement

### Disclaimer type
- Set in Chevy Sans (regular or narrow)
- Minimum point size per platform specified in brand guide
- Color: high contrast against background

### "From" / MSRP / lease pricing
- Lease offers must show: payment, term, down payment, residual, MSRP, lessee total at signing, disposition fee, mileage cap
- APR offers must show: APR %, term, qualifying conditions, expiration
- Tax/title/license language standard

## 8. Film Cutdowns (Retail)

The 2025 brand guide standardizes 4 retail film cutdown patterns. When producing retail TV/digital video for Chevrolet:

1. **:20/:7/:3**, long, medium, short trio
2. **:17/:10/:3**, alternative long/medium/short
3. **:12/:3**, single medium + short bumper
4. **:5/:7/:3**, three short cuts for high-frequency placement

Choose the cutdown set based on placement strategy. Each cutdown must include the legal disclaimer dwell-time rule (Section 7).

## 11. Rejection Checklist

Items that get flagged in Chevrolet co-op / AND Chevrolet campaign review:

- [ ] Outlined (2D) bowtie used where 3D was viable
- [ ] Bowtie under minimum size
- [ ] Bowtie altered (colors, rotation, effects)
- [ ] Wordmark + bowtie spacing wrong
- [ ] TLD tagline spacing wrong, or missing trademark symbol
- [ ] Wrong typography (not Chevy Sans, or wrong weight for the use)
- [ ] Headline not ALL CAPS where it should be
- [ ] Model name written incorrectly (lowercase, invented abbreviation)
- [ ] Disclaimer dwell time under 5 seconds in video
- [ ] Pricing offer missing required component (residual, mileage cap, etc.)
- [ ] Damaged or dirty vehicle in standard retail creative
- [ ] Copy covering the vehicle
- [ ] Co-brand lockup not balanced or not on approved list

## 12. Quick Compliance Checklist

- [ ] **Logo:** 3D gold bowtie used (outlined only if production-justified)
- [ ] **Logo size:** Above minimum (horizontal ≥ 150px, vertical ≥ 78px)
- [ ] **Logo clear space:** ≥ 1 wing-height on all sides
- [ ] **Tagline:** "Together let's drive" with trademark, correct spacing
- [ ] **Typography:** Chevy Sans (correct family + weight per use)
- [ ] **Headlines:** ALL CAPS, Chevy Sans Black
- [ ] **Vehicle naming:** Correct (CHEVROLET SILVERADO in headlines, Silverado in body)
- [ ] **Photography:** Vehicle clean, hero, no damage, no obstruction
- [ ] **Disclaimer dwell:** ≥ 5 seconds for video
- [ ] **Pricing:** All required components present
- [ ] **No prohibited treatments:** No altered bowtie, no rotated/recolored marks
- [ ] **Co-brand lockup:** On approved list, optically balanced
- [ ] **Film cutdowns:** Match one of the 4 sanctioned patterns
- [ ] **Seasonal logos:** Only approved Chevrolet seasonal lockups, no dealer-custom
