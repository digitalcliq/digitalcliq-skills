## Scope of this policy

This is primarily a **MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) policy**, not a full visual identity guide. It governs how dealers may advertise new, untitled Harley motorcycles in designated media. Logo / typography / color rules for HD are covered by a separate brand book (not currently in vault). When working on HD creative beyond pricing/disclosure language, request the HD brand book or escalate to dealer for guidance.

---

## 1. Coverage

### Covered products
- **New, untitled MY2025 and MY2026** Harley-Davidson motorcycles
- All families: Touring, Softail, Sportster, CVO, Adventure Touring, Trike, etc.
- MAP does not break out by family; if it is a current model-year new bike, it is covered

### NOT covered
- MY2024 (no longer covered as of October 1, 2025)
- Used or pre-owned bikes
- Rentals
- Parts and accessories (covered by a separate P&A MAP policy)
- MotorClothes

### Covered media
"Designated media" per the policy. Any place a dealer publicly communicates pricing: dealer website, paid search ads, social posts, third-party listing sites (Cycle Trader, Facebook Marketplace), print ads, OOH, radio, TV, email marketing, in-store signage with displayed pricing.

## 2. The MAP itself

### Default rule
**MAP = MSRP** for the stock model in base color.

### Named exceptions (effective November 3)
| Model | MAP |
|---|---|
| 2025 Street Glide | $26,999 |
| 2025 Heritage Classic | $21,999 |
| 2025 Low Rider ST | $23,999 |
| 2025 Breakout | $22,999 |

These 4 models have explicit dollar MAPs that may differ from MSRP. Verify against the source PDF before quoting any specific number; HD updates these periodically.

### What MAP excludes
MAP is the MSRP excluding:
- Tax
- Title and licensing
- Registration
- Destination
- Accessories
- Additional dealer charges (doc fees, prep, etc.)

### Adjusting for non-base configurations
If the advertised bike has non-base paint or factory-installed options:
- Roll those MSRPs into the advertised price
- Update the disclaimer to match
- Net advertised price still cannot drop below the base MAP

## 3. What you CANNOT do

### Prohibited price representations
- Cannot advertise a price **below MAP**
- Cannot include discounts, rebates, credits, or any price reduction that drops the net advertised price below MAP
- Cannot use phrasing that implies sub-MAP pricing:
  - "20% off List Price"
  - "Clearance Sale"
  - "Huge Markdowns"
  - "Below MAP pricing"
  - "Lowest price in [region]"
  - "Make us an offer"
  - "Beat any deal"

### Prohibited phrases: pricing inquiry alternatives
These are explicitly banned phrases (cannot pair with any displayed price):
- "E-Price"
- "Internet Price"
- "Call for Best Price"
- "Special Internet Pricing"
- Any phrase implying a lower price is available by contacting the dealer

### Allowed pricing inquiry phrasing
- "Call for Price": allowed only when **no price is listed**
- If a price IS shown alongside "Call for Price" or similar, it becomes a violation

## 4. Value-added offers (allowed if structured correctly)

You CAN advertise these without violating MAP:

### Allowed value-adds
- Free labor on accessory install
- Free MotorClothes or P&A
- Trade-in values (no maximum specified on trade allowance)
- Free service / maintenance
- Free or low-interest financing
- "No money down"
- Delayed payments

### Caps on stated value
- **$200 cap** on any stated dollar value of a value-added offer or gift card
- OR omit the dollar value entirely (e.g., "Free 5,000-mile service" without a dollar amount)

### Gift card rules
- Allowed: gift cards for P&A, General Merchandise, or services
- **NOT allowed:** gift cards redeemable toward the new motorcycle purchase
- Ad must disclose that the gift card cannot be used toward the bike

### Customer referral bonuses
- Allowed
- Ad must use the word "referral" or similar ("bring a friend", "tell a friend")

## 5. Penalties

### Per-violation fine
- **$750 per vehicle retailed** for 3 months following the violation
- **Capped at $75,000** per violation
- One documented violation triggers this, there is no "warning" tier

### Cumulative enforcement
- Pattern of violations can compound
- Severe or repeated violations risk dealer agreement consequences (refer to dealer franchise agreement)

---

## 6. Pre-approval workflow

### Before any HD ad ships
- Submit to the **MAP portal on H-Dnet** for pre-approval
- Do not assume "obvious compliance" lets you skip the portal
- Portal approval is documentation if a complaint is filed later

### Documentation to retain
- Screenshot or PDF of every published ad
- MAP portal approval confirmation
- Effective dates of any promotional window

## 7. Common violation patterns

Things flagged in real-world HD enforcement actions:

- [ ] **Discount language combined with price:** "$22,500 (was $24,999)" on a Street Glide
- [ ] **Internet Price or E-Price label** anywhere on a dealer site product listing
- [ ] **"Call for best price"** displayed below a listed MSRP
- [ ] **Stacked rebates** that net the advertised price below MAP
- [ ] **Gift card amounts over $200** stated explicitly
- [ ] **Gift cards advertised as redeemable toward the bike**
- [ ] **Sub-MAP "Make Offer" auctions** on third-party listing sites (still a violation even on auctioneer's platform if the dealer placed the listing)
- [ ] **MY2024 priced as if covered by current MAP**: MY2024 is no longer in scope, but the dealer should not represent old-MY pricing as a MAP-driven promo
- [ ] **Missing disclaimer** about gift card restrictions

---

## 8. Disclaimer template (HD pricing ads)

When advertising a price near or above MAP with a value-add bonus, suggested disclaimer pattern (verify against current HD legal templates):

> Advertised price excludes tax, title, license, registration, destination, dealer charges, and any optional equipment. [Value-add item] has a stated value not to exceed $200 and is not redeemable toward the purchase of the motorcycle. Offer valid [dates] at participating Harley-Davidson dealers. See dealer for details.

---

## 9. Quick Compliance Checklist

- [ ] **Coverage check:** Bike is MY2025 or MY2026, new, untitled
- [ ] **MAP correct:** Advertised price ≥ MAP for that model (using the 4 named exceptions where applicable)
- [ ] **No discount language:** No "% off," no "below invoice," no "clearance," no markdown framing
- [ ] **No banned price-inquiry phrases:** No "E-Price," "Internet Price," "Call for Best Price"
- [ ] **"Call for Price" only when no price is shown**
- [ ] **Value-adds capped at $200** if dollar value is stated
- [ ] **Gift cards** restricted to P&A/General/services AND disclosed as non-redeemable toward bike
- [ ] **Trade-in / financing language** structured per allowed forms
- [ ] **Pre-approved through MAP portal on H-Dnet**
- [ ] **Documentation retained:** ad screenshot + portal approval
- [ ] **Disclaimer present** for exclusions and value-add restrictions
- [ ] **MY2024 not represented as MAP-covered** (out of scope since Oct 1 2025)

---

## 10. When MAP doesn't apply

If the ad does NOT advertise a specific price, MAP is not triggered. Brand-awareness, lifestyle, and event advertising for HD that omits pricing entirely sits outside MAP enforcement. Beware: showing inventory with prices on the dealer website is still "advertising" under MAP, even if the homepage is purely brand creative.
