# 2026 Social Growth Playbook (skill knowledge base)

Distilled from live 2026 research. This is the strategy brain behind the 30/60/90
plans. Refresh annually. **Hashtags are not a growth strategy in 2026** on any
platform: they are a weak categorization label. Discovery is driven by (a) social
SEO / keywords in captions, on-screen text, spoken audio, and profile fields, and
(b) high-intent engagement signals (sends, saves, shares, replays, watch-time,
replies). Build for those.

## The universal 2026 truth

Reach now comes from **reach-to-non-followers signals**, not follower count:
- **Sends / shares (DMing a post to a friend)** = the single strongest reach lever everywhere.
- **Saves / bookmarks** = ~3x a like; the "I'll come back to this" vote.
- **Watch-time / completion / replays** = the gate that decides wide vs throttled.
- **Replies + author-replies in the first hour** = early-velocity multiplier.
- **Original, person-led, lo-fi video** beats polished brand spots and reposts.

To **2x followers + interactions**, you 2x *reach per post* (the signals above) and
hold a consistent cadence. Interactions double faster than followers, interactions
are directly controllable (cadence, format shift to short video, replying to every
comment, CTAs). Treat follower-doubling as the stretch, interaction-doubling as the floor.

## Instagram: relationship + local SEO engine
- **Rewards:** watch-time/retention, **sends per reach** (3-5x a like), saves (~3x), ratio-likes, conversation depth, **original content** (reposters lose 60-80% reach), caption/audio/alt-text keywords (IG SEO), first-30-60-min velocity.
- **Formats:** Reels are discovery. 7-15s for reach, 15-30s educational, up to 3 min now recommended to non-followers if retention holds. 9:16, in-app text/captions, no watermarks. Carousels for saves/depth. Interactive Stories daily. **Trial Reels** to A/B hooks on non-followers.
- **Cadence:** 3-7 Reels/week + daily Stories + 1-3 carousels/week. Post Tue/Wed/Thu midday-evening (Thu 9am, Wed 12pm/6pm strong). A 10-day gap resets reach.
- **Levers:** explicit "send this to someone shopping for a [truck]" CTAs; bookmark-bait (comparisons, checklists); win first 3s + watch-time; caption SEO in first 125 chars; reply within the hour; **Collab posts** with local accounts; launch a **Broadcast Channel**; keyword-optimized name field + bio.
- **Auto angle:** localize (geo tags, city keywords, local "shoulder content"); sell the people not the metal; inventory as save/send bait; Broadcast Channel for VIP inventory drops; caption SEO for "[Year][Make][Model] for sale in [City]" (now indexed by Google too); local Collabs (detailers, lenders, sports teams).

## TikTok: highest ceiling, the viral lane (LAUNCH first if absent)
- **Rewards:** completion/replays, **shares + saves**, comments/replies, search relevance. A 200-follower dealer can reach thousands of local buyers in 24h. ~99% video, 9:16 only.
- **Benchmarks:** avg ER 3.4-4.9%; under-5k accounts run 4.2%+; dealer "good" = 4-6%. Doubling followers organically is a 3-6 month project; a single breakout can double a small account in days.
- **Formats:** two lengths win, sub-30s high-completion hooks AND 1-3 min retention holders. Hook in first 1-3s (96% of reach lands in first ~10 days). Carousels/photo+video mixes gaining. **Recurring series** (3-4 themes) so the algorithm classifies you. Authentic, person-led, lightly produced.
- **Cadence:** 3-5/week (top 25% post 4+), 4-6h gap. Tue-Thu 10-11am + 2-6pm local. Consistency > volume.
- **Levers:** engineer quotable/loopable/saveable; reply-with-video; Duets/Stitches/collabs; lock a content lane; TikTok SEO every post; go LIVE once traction; weekly analytics editing loop.
- **Auto angle:** sub-30s single-vehicle walkaround with year/make/model + price + 2-3 features spoken AND on-screen (feeds the algorithm exact match data + ranks in search); price-reveal hook ("How much is a 2026 RAM 1500?" → cut to number); same-day new-arrivals; before/after recon; salesperson-led; local SEO in caption+text+voiceover. Organic CPL ~20-40% of paid search.

## YouTube: evergreen search/SEO asset + Shorts discovery
- **Rewards:** average-view-duration + satisfaction, **shares**, early comment velocity, session contribution. Measure watch-time, not ER.
- **Formats:** Shorts 30-45s (sub-15s collapsed), aim 50%+ AVD; long-form 8-15 min, 60%+ AVD, custom thumbnail + keyword in first 65 chars of title, **chapters** on every long-form. The **Shorts→long-form flywheel** lifts long-form watch-time ~40%.
- **Cadence:** 1-2 long-form/week (2/week grows subs ~3x faster) + 3-5 Shorts/week, fixed times. Long-form Thu-Fri 2-4pm; Shorts 12-3pm + 7-9pm.
- **Levers:** engineer for shares; run the flywheel; **Collaboration feature** (tag up to 4 channels); win first 24-48h; pin a question; cold opens for AVD; Hype + Community posts; repurpose one shoot into 4-6 Shorts.
- **Auto angle:** own "near me" intent (searches doubled into 2026, 92% of shoppers research on YT); education-first (6 in 10 start unsure); inventory-velocity Shorts with VIN CTA; real salespeople/GM as recurring faces (78% trust creators); local CTA + retargeting; local collabs; recurring series ("New Arrivals Friday", "Just Got Traded", monthly "Lease Deals Explained").

## Facebook: local community + older buyer + lead reach
- **Rewards:** sends/private shares (top signal), saves, Reels watch-time (only format reaching non-followers), replies. Organic reach ~1.65% but high-relevance posts hit ~18.6%.
- **Benchmarks:** ER ~0.15-1.3%; "good" 1-2%. Don't set an aggressive ER target; set reach/lead targets. ~100-500 new followers/month realistic.
- **Formats:** Reels 15-30s 9:16 (~135% more reach than photos); lo-fi mobile video; photo carousels for saves; single photos for community ER; daily Stories; Broadcast Channels at 10k (50-80% open). Short keyword-rich front-loaded captions.
- **Cadence:** 3-5 feed + 4-6 Reels/week + daily Stories. Tue/Wed/Thu, 9-11am + 12-1pm feed, 5-9pm Reels.
- **Levers:** shift mix to Reels; engineer sends/saves; reply within the hour + ask a question; **Collabs/co-posts** with local partners; Facebook-SEO the whole Page; Broadcast Channel + Groups; boost proven organic Reels (Conversions API, first-party data); 6+ weeks before judging; avoid engagement-bait (can cut reach 80%).
- **Auto angle:** salesperson-led 60-90s vertical walkarounds (weekly series); win the local interest graph; UGC + delivery-day (~4.5x higher engagement); tie every video to ONE conversion action; Broadcast Channel for "first dibs"; local Collabs; build around brand/process/experience so content survives staff turnover.

## X / Threads: top-of-funnel familiarity (low priority for a single store)
- **Rewards:** reply-and-bookmark rate (not likes). X: author-reply weight (+75 highest lever), bookmarks (+10, 5x a like), reposts (~20x a like). Threads: replies-per-reach (~30 per 1k reach is good); 20+ replies in first 30 min gets expanded.
- **Formats:** X text-only posts out-engage video/images (~30% higher); 71-100 char sweet spot; threads (4-8 posts) get ~3x engagement; native video 15-60s 9:16 captioned; **never put the link in the main post** (put in a reply). Threads is text-first but +1 clean photo lifts reach ~60%; Hook→Context→Position→Invitation; 1 Topic Tag.
- **Cadence:** X 3-6/day scaling to 10+, Tue-Thu 9am-3pm. Threads 1-3/day, weekday 7-9am + 6-9pm (Thu 9am strongest), daily for first 60 days.
- **Levers:** reply to your repliers within the hour; spend 10-15 min/day replying under bigger accounts (most follows come via replies); engineer bookmarks/saves; reposts/sends; pick one lane 60-90 days; X Premium (2-4x reach); cross-pollinate IG↔Threads (~15% faster).
- **Auto angle:** salesperson as face (72% of teams use video); unscripted 30s walkarounds (10x reach of long reviews); local search in first caption line; delivery-photo engine; jump into local conversations; bookmark-bait specials; route conversion via reply/DM (never main-post link); ≤1 of every 5-6 posts is hard promo.

## The 8 dealership content pillars (rotate these)
1. **Inventory walkarounds & new arrivals**: highest-ROI; 9:16 15-45s; specs + price spoken + on-screen.
2. **Price reveals & deals**: "guess the payment" curiosity gap; OEM/CA-compliant.
3. **Staff personality / salesperson-as-creator**: build 1-2 internal faces; people follow people.
4. **Education / insider tips**: F&I, trade-in, financing, "what dealers don't tell you"; saves + YT SEO.
5. **Behind-the-scenes & before/after**: recon/detailing transformations, delivery reveals.
6. **Community & local**: events, sponsorships, staff spotlights; anchors Facebook.
7. **UGC & reviews**: repost happy customers, delivery photos, review screenshots.
8. **Trends, memes & participation**: trending audio with an inventory twist; sparingly, on-brand.
